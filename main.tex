

\begin{abstract}
    In this article we will be exploring development and creation of accessible documents using \LaTeX.\/ This will include commonly used commands, types of mathematical equations, imported images, TiKz images, and tables.  Parts of the document will also test the effects of making multiple columns and using tcolorboxes.  Ultimately we will be trying to maximize the accessibility of the generated pdf, but acknowleding the inherent shortcomings of pdf documents, we will also be looking to generate HTML directly from the source code using Pandoc, \TeX 4ht, and \LaTeX ML.
\end{abstract}
